# "standard" skeleton — English

This file is **guidance**, not a slot template. Each section declares its
*intent*, its *register* and a *length budget*; the model writes freely within
them. No sample sentence appears here: a sample sentence would become, on the
first generation, a sentence of the letter.

Two rules carry everything else:

- **No claim about the candidate that does not come from the supplied facts.**
  The supplied facts are the ones `select_evidence` retained, and nothing else.
- **This skeleton is not a translation of `standard.fr.md`, nor the reverse.**
  Each language has its own letter; none is machine-translated. English-language
  cover letters are shorter and less formal than French ones, and the budgets
  below reflect that — they are not the French numbers converted.

The editorial content of the intents below is **open to human revision**: it
carries the tone of the application, not a technical constraint.

## opening
- intention: Name the role and the company exactly as the posting names them, and state in one sentence what motivates the application. No flattery about the company, no fact about it that does not come from the posting.
- registre: hérité
- budget_mots: 40

## evidence
- intention: Develop the supplied facts, one per paragraph, tying each to a requirement of the role. Stay within what the facts say; do not extrapolate a duration, a team size, a figure or a scope of responsibility that is not in them.
- registre: hérité
- budget_mots: 160

## fit
- intention: State what those facts make it possible to bring to this role. This is an avowed projection, not a factual claim: phrase it as an intention, never as a past achievement.
- registre: hérité
- budget_mots: 60

## closing
- intention: Offer a conversation and close plainly. No commitment on availability, notice period or salary expectation that is not in the supplied facts.
- registre: neutre
- budget_mots: 40
